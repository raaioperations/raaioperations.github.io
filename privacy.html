Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

RAAI Operations develops focused software products, including local-first applications. This policy explains the information our website and products may handle and the principles we apply to that information.

1. Website data

This website is designed as a static informational site. It does not intentionally use advertising trackers, marketing analytics, account systems, or third-party behavioral tracking.

2. Product data

Some RAAI Operations products are designed to store user-created information locally in the user's browser or device. Where a product operates locally, that product data is not intentionally transmitted to RAAI Operations unless a feature clearly requires a network service and the user chooses to use it.

3. Payment information

If RAAI Operations offers paid software through an authorized payment or merchant-of-record provider, payment information such as card numbers and security codes is handled by that provider rather than by this static website or the local application itself.

4. Support communications

If you contact support, we may receive the information you choose to provide in that communication. We use it to respond to your request, investigate product issues, and maintain necessary business records.

5. Data sales

RAAI Operations does not represent its business model as selling customer estimate content or user-created product data.

6. Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection and infrastructure. No system is completely secure, and users should maintain appropriate backups of important local data where a product provides backup functionality.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as products, services, or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: raaioperationssupport@gmail.com
