Decision gate

Idea → demand → regulatory feasibility → commercial feasibility → build → sell → profit → ownership → scale. For each candidate record buyer, price, time to revenue, cost, recurring potential, ownership, risk, evidence, and next action. Capture ideas without automatically authorizing development.

Action classifications: EXECUTE · MONETIZE · MAINTAIN · INCUBATE · ARCHIVE · TERMINATE. RESTRUCTURE and FOUNDATION are portfolio-level flags. Unreviewed means no commercial decision has been made.

02 / Risk and compliance

Regulatory classification

Internal screening rubric, not a government standard or legal determination. US/California baseline assumption; jurisdiction and actual operating model must be confirmed. R0 does not mean exempt. R4 blocks the proposed model, not necessarily lawful alternatives.

R0Minimal / internal24Internal or ordinary low-burden activity
R1Standard compliance38Baseline business, tax, privacy and consumer obligations
R2Specialized review11Industry-specific obligations or professional review
R3Heavy regulation2Potential licensing, approvals or significant oversight
R4Blocked model0Do not proceed under the blocked model
R?Unclassified5Scope or evidence insufficient

Counts are registry entries, not distinct legal entities. Classifications describe the stated scope, including explicit escalation gates; changed models must be re-screened. No entry is marked legally cleared. Before sale: confirm jurisdiction, registration, permits/licenses, tax, data/privacy, IP, liability/insurance, provider terms, costs and evidence from authoritative sources.
