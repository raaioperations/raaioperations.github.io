Operating doctrine

Workflow comes first.

Before optimizing an individual product, optimize the machinery that builds products: specifications, reusable components, agent orchestration, verification, deployment, recovery, observability, and canonical roadmaps. A foundation that makes every later build faster, safer, and more capable compounds across the entire portfolio.
