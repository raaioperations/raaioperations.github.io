Projects are ranked primarily by foundational leverage: how strongly each capability improves shared workflow, autonomous building, verification, reuse, deployment, and the speed and reliability of future projects.

Operating doctrine

Workflow comes first.

Build a capability once and reuse it everywhere. Every project must consume the shared foundation or improve it; duplicated solutions trigger architectural reconsideration. Prioritize specifications, reusable components, agent orchestration, independent verification, deployment, recovery, observability, and canonical roadmaps. Shared improvements compound across the entire portfolio.
