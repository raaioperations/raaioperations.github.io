About RadioScope

RadioScope V1.2 is a local-first directory and player for publicly listed internet-radio stations, a remote-SDR link hub, and a phone-microphone sound analyzer.

It cannot detect arbitrary radio signals, police radios, nearby transmissions, Wi-Fi networks, or Bluetooth devices on your phone. Internet scanning cycles through streams listed in the directory, not RF channels.

Station data: Radio Browser community directory. Streams and remote SDR websites are provided by independent operators and may be offline or subject to terms. Use only legally accessible services.
